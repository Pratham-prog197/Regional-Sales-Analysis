# Sales Analytics & Business Insights Dashboard

## Project Overview
This project focuses on end-to-end sales analytics using Python and Power BI. The objective was to analyze regional sales performance, customer behavior, product profitability, and channel contribution using historical business data.

The project involved data cleaning, feature engineering, exploratory data analysis (EDA), dashboard development, and business insight generation.

## Tools & Technologies
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Power BI
- Jupyter Notebook

 ## Why I Chose This Project

Businesses often generate massive amounts of sales data, but without proper analysis, it becomes difficult to identify:
- High-performing products
- Profitable customers
- Strong and weak regions
- Seasonal sales trends
- Channel effectiveness

I chose this project to gain hands-on experience with real-world business analytics workflows and understand 
how data can be used to support business growth and operational decision-making.

This project also helped me strengthen my skills in:
- Python for data analysis
- Data cleaning and transformation
- Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- Business KPI generation
- Dashboard development using Power BI
- Data storytelling and business recommendations

## Business Problem Statement

Sales teams and business stakeholders often lack a centralized, data-driven view of performance across:
- Products
- Regions
- Customers
- Sales channels

This creates challenges in:
- Identifying growth opportunities
- Improving profitability
- Understanding customer behavior
- Optimizing inventory and pricing strategies

The goal of this project was to analyze 5 years of historical sales data to uncover trends, identify key revenue drivers, and support data-driven business decisions.


## Project Workflow
1. Data Collection & Understanding
2. Data Cleaning & Preprocessing
3. Data Merging & Transformation
4. Feature Engineering
5. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
6. Business Insights Generation
7. Power BI Dashboard Creation

## Key KPIs
- Total Revenue
- Total Profit
- Profit Margin %
- Average Order Value
- Regional Sales Performance
- Channel Contribution

  # Problem Solved

This project helps solve multiple business challenges by:
- Identifying top-performing products and regions
- Understanding sales seasonality trends
- Measuring channel profitability
- Analyzing customer contribution to revenue
- Detecting low-performing areas for improvement
- Supporting strategic planning through interactive dashboards

The final dashboard enables business users to:
- Monitor KPIs in real time
- Compare regional performance
- Track profitability trends
- Explore customer segmentation
- Make informed business decisions

## Key Insights
- Wholesale channel contributed over 54% of total sales.
- Export channel generated the highest average profit margins.
- California was the top-performing state by revenue and order volume.
- Products 25 and 26 were the highest revenue-generating SKUs.
- Strong seasonality patterns were observed across monthly sales.
## Recommendations
- Expand high-margin export partnerships.
- Improve performance of low-margin SKUs.
- Increase regional investment in underperforming areas.
- Use seasonal campaigns to stabilize revenue fluctuations.
